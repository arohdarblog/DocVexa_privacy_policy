Privacy Policy

DocVexa

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

🔒 Summary: DocVexa is designed with a strict privacy-first philosophy. All document viewing, PDF conversion, image cropping, and Excel calculations happen entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal information or document files.

1. Information We Collect

DocVexa does not collect any personal identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address to use the app.
  • No File Uploads: When you open a PDF, view an Excel file, or convert photos to PDF, all processing is performed locally on your device. Your documents never leave your phone.
  • No Tracking / Analytics: We do not track your location, browsing habits, device identifiers, or usage patterns.

2. Device Permissions Used

DocVexa only requests permissions that are strictly necessary to provide its core features:

  • Camera Permission (Optional): Used solely when you choose to capture physical documents using the camera to convert them into a PDF. Images captured by the camera are processed exclusively on your device and are never shared with any remote server.
  • Storage / Media: The app utilizes Android's zero-permission system Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia) and Storage Access Framework. This ensures the app never requests broad access to your photo library or private folders. Generated PDFs are saved in the app's secure private directory and can be saved or shared at your discretion.

3. Offline & On-Device Security

All core functionalities—including rendering complex PDF pages, computing Excel formulas, and auto-cropping document edges—run completely offline using native Android APIs. The app functions without requiring an active internet connection.

4. Third-Party Services

DocVexa does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, data brokers, or behavioral analytics services. Your documents, photos, and device state remain private and isolated.

5. Children's Privacy

DocVexa does not address or knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app is a general productivity tool safe for all audiences.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page and reflected within the app's in-app Privacy Policy viewer.
